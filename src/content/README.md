# `src/content` — shipped reference text

Content the app **renders** rather than computes with, bundled into the build
instead of seeded into the data folder.

`assumptions/` is the other kind: dials the owner is invited to edit, copied
into their folder on first run so their numbers are theirs. This directory is
the opposite bargain, and one file has earned it so far.

**`widow-playbook.json`** — the conventional half of the Widow's Playbook.
Every item carrying a number or a deadline (the $255 lump sum and its two-year
window, the nine-month disclaimer, Form 706 portability, SSA-44) is a claim
about the law that goes stale exactly the way a tax bracket does, and it is
read under stress by somebody who cannot check it. A copy frozen in a data
folder seeded years ago is therefore a liability rather than a courtesy: this
text must update when the app does. Sources and the verification date are in
VERIFICATIONS.md under "Widow's Playbook", the same discipline the tax tables
are held to.
